Available Kits

Until now, pod cars were unattainable to all but big features and shows that could justify a single-vehicle use. Through the innovative thinking of a team that has revolutionized picture-vehicle methods for a quarter century, that has changed. Our noninvasive kits are Model-based, so countless stock cars can be day-played as pod cars.
